Production

How every ad gets made, step by step.

Seven stages behind every ad, the same for every style. Nothing goes to edit until each stage is checked.

01Research

Brief & reference teardown

I study your product, audience and claims, then break down a winning reference ad: hook, pacing, angles and why it converts.

Output · Production brief
02Look

Style lock

I pick one look up front, like cinematic drama, Pixar 3D, claymation, UGC or animated, and lock lighting, palette and lens so every shot matches.

Output · Style frame
03Cast

Characters & locations

Character sheets and location plates built as references, so the same face, outfit and set carry through every scene.

Output · Character + location sheets
04Plan

Shot list & prompts

A shot direction table: shot size, angle, camera move and acting for every line, plus the B-roll, all written as generation prompts.

Output · Shot list + prompt sheet
05Make

Image & video generation

Keyframes first, then clips in Kling, Seedance, Higgsfield and others. Each clip is checked for faces, hands, product and continuity, and redone if it fails.

Output · Approved clips
06Sound

Voice, lip-sync & music

Voiceover or dialogue, lip-sync, an original song for song ads, plus SFX and music mixed to sit under the voice.

Output · Audio tracks
07Deliver

QA & export

A final check of sound levels, captions, sync and the product shot, then export in the formats you need for Meta, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

Output · Launch-ready files
